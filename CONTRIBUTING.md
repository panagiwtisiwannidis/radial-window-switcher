# Contributing to Radial Switcher

Thanks for your interest! Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are welcome.

## Before you contribute

Radial Switcher is not open source: it is owned by Panagiotis Ioannidis and all
rights are reserved (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). So that the project stays under a
single owner, contributions are accepted on these terms:

By submitting a contribution (code, documentation, images or anything else) to
this repository, for example by opening a pull request, you:

1. confirm that you wrote it yourself, or otherwise have the right to submit it;
2. assign to Panagiotis Ioannidis all copyright and other rights in your
   contribution, or, where an assignment is not possible under applicable law,
   grant him an exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free
   license to use, modify, sublicense and distribute it in any way, including
   commercially;
3. understand that your contribution may be changed, included or left out at
   his discretion, and that you receive no payment or other rights for it.

If you do not agree, please open an issue describing the change instead of
sending code.

## How to contribute

- **Bugs and ideas**: open an issue with your OS version, what you did, what you
  expected and what happened.
- **Pull requests**: keep each one focused on a single change, match the style
  of the surrounding code (no code comments), and make sure the build and lints
  pass for the platform you touched (see the README of each platform).
